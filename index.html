ASCII Table & Developer Tools

An ASCII table plus tools for text, encodings, Base64, URL and HTML escaping, JWT, JSON, diffs, regex, numbers, time and hashes. Everything runs in your browser. Nothing you paste or open here is uploaded.

Ctrl+Enter (⌘+Enter on a Mac) runs the tool you're typing in.

Click any cell to copy it. With the keyboard, move with the arrow keys and press Enter to copy.
